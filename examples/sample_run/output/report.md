# Quillfeather Robotics: company intelligence report

**Publish decision: publish.** Coverage and verification thresholds were met.

11 claims kept, 5 suppressed by verification. 7 of 23 captured sources are cited.

## Executive summary

Quillfeather Robotics is a venture-backed maker of offshore wind inspection drones growing revenue 31% to $48.2 million while still loss-making. A pending patent suit, a January 2026 drone recall and 28% revenue concentration in one customer are the main risks.

## Key findings

- Revenue grew 31% to $48.2 million in FY2025; the company is not profitable.
- A patent infringement case filed by Aerodyne Systems is pending.
- One customer accounts for 28% of revenue.
